What we heard

The brief

An Android TV app that removes official YouTube, keeps it removed, and keeps TizenTube installed and current. It should feel calm, solid and protective, and match the app's dark Material 3 Expressive screens.

calmsolidprotectivemodern

Success: no shield or padlock cliché, nothing that resembles YouTube's mark, recognisable at 16 px and on a TV launcher, works in one colour.

Concept A

Seal

The scalloped shape from the app's own screens becomes a seal of protection, with play cut out of its centre.

  • Protection told by a seal, not the usual shield or padlock
  • The same shape language as the app's Material 3 Expressive UI
  • Bold silhouette and a large play: reads at 16 px and across the room on a TV
